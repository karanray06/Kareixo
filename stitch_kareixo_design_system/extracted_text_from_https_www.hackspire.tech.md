## HackSpire'26

HackSpire'26, organized by the FIEM ACM Student Chapter of Future Institute of Engineering and Management (FIEM), is among Kolkata's top free student hackathons for October 2026 — a 26-hour in-person sprint on 2–3 October where innovation meets the cultural essence of Durga Puja. Building on 4,100+ HackSpire'25 registrations and FIEM ACM's recognition as the Most Active ACM Student Chapter in East India, it unites developers, designers, and problem-solvers nationwide under the theme Where Innovation Meets Shakti. Join us for our most immersive edition yet.

## Frequently Asked  
Questions

01

### What is HackSpire'26 and how does it work?

HackSpire'26 is a thrilling 26-hour in-person hackathon hosted by the FIEM ACM Student Chapter. Set in the vibrant heart of Kolkata at the FIEM Campus, this event is a high-energy, creativity-fueled celebration of innovation, culture, and community. Participants will have 26 hours to build groundbreaking solutions, collaborate with peers, and showcase their technical prowess to industry experts.

02

### How do I register for the hackathon?

Registration is simple and completely free of cost! Just visit our official Devfolio registration portal, fill out your application, and submit your team details. Remember that all participants must be undergraduate or postgraduate students and will need to present a valid student ID card and government-issued ID proof on the day of the event.

03

### Can I participate online?

No, HackSpire'26 is strictly an in-person offline hackathon. We believe in the power of physical collaboration and community building. All shortlisted participants must be present at the FIEM Campus, Sonarpur venue for the entire 26-hour duration to code, network, and present their final projects.

04

### What is the team size limit?

Teams must consist of 2 to 4 members. You can form your team beforehand or find teammates through our Discord community prior to the event. Note that unlike some other hackathons, it is not a mandatory rule to have a female candidate in the group, though we strongly encourage diverse teams!

05

### Will accommodation and food be provided?

Yes! Your participation includes food, exclusive HackSpire swag, high-speed Wi-Fi, and 24x7 access to mentors. Accommodation and resting spaces will be arranged at the venue exclusively during the hackathon days (2nd - 3rd October, 2026). Just bring your laptops, chargers, and enthusiasm!
